Privacy Policy

Workout Log · Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Workout Log runs entirely in your browser. It reads and writes only the Google Sheet you choose, it has no server, and the developer never receives, stores or sees your data.

What Workout Log is

Workout Log is a free, personal web app for logging gym workouts into your own Google Sheets workout tracker. It is a static web page hosted on GitHub Pages and maintained by an individual developer (GitHub: yahia-raheem). It is not a company product and has no ads or paid features.

Google account access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for a single permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: access only to the specific Google Drive files you open with the app (for example, the sheet you choose in the Google file picker). The app cannot see, list or open any other files in your Drive.

The app uses this access to:

  • read the Plan, Settings and Historical Data tabs of your chosen sheet to check its format, show your workout plan, exercise videos and previous results;
  • read the Historical Data and (if present) Body Data tabs to calculate the charts and summaries on the Insights screen. These calculations run in your browser and the results are not stored anywhere;
  • add new rows to the Historical Data tab when you save a workout (and add empty rows to that tab if it is full).

The app does not delete or change your existing workout rows, and it does not create, share or delete files.

In PT mode only, the app can also rewrite the sheet's Plan tab when the PT saves a training plan, and add a Plan Archive tab to keep a copy of a replaced plan. It shows a summary of these changes before writing them.

In PT mode the app can also write a weekly cardio target next to the plan name on the Plan tab. The PT can also save targets (reps, weight and a note per set) for each day of the plan in a PT Targets tab. The trainee's app reads that tab while a workout is being logged and marks that day's targets as used once the workout is saved.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account information. Google's sign-in window may show you your account, but that information is not passed to or stored by Workout Log.

Where your data goes

  • Your workout data is sent directly from your browser to Google's APIs (Google Sheets API, Google Drive / Picker API) and stored only in your own Google Sheet. It never passes through any server run by the developer.
  • Sign-in tokens from Google are kept in your browser's memory for the current session only (about one hour) and are discarded when you close the page or sign out.
  • On your device, the app uses your browser's local storage to remember the last sheet you opened (its ID and name), the last workout day you selected, and unsaved workout entries so you don't lose them if the page reloads, and, if you unlocked PT mode, that PT mode is on for this device. This data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page.

The rest timer runs on your device. If you allow notifications, they are created on your device by your browser when a rest period ends; nothing is sent to a server. While a rest timer runs, the app asks the browser to keep the screen on. A running timer is remembered in your browser's local storage so it survives a page reload.

If you install the app to your home screen, a small background script (service worker) is installed with it. It is only used to show notifications and does not store or send any data.

Plan files exported in PT mode are created on your device and go only where you choose to save or share them.

Workout Log has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and sets no cookies of its own.

Third-party services

  • Google provides sign-in, the file picker and the Sheets API. Your use of them is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: when you tap an exercise's video button, the video is loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own policies apply to that video.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses when the page is loaded; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Workout Log's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the app's features described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else and not read by any person other than you.

Removing access

You can sign out from the app's menu at any time, which revokes the app's access token. To remove the app's access completely, go to your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page and remove Workout Log. Your sheet and everything in it stay in your Google Drive under your control.

Children

Workout Log is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes
